Privacy & data

We use your email address and question only to handle your request and send you a personal reply. We never publish your story without your permission. No account or public name is required.

The form itself does not send or store your message on this website: after you continue, you must open your email app and press Send. Your email provider and our email provider process the correspondence. Payment is handled by PayPal when you use its checkout.

Our policy is to delete advice emails from our active mailbox within 14 days after your conversation is complete. For packages with follow-ups, we wait until the follow-up period has ended. You can request earlier deletion by writing to contact@anonymadvice.com. This process is currently handled manually.

We use Google Analytics only if you accept analytics cookies. You can change your preference using Cookie settings below.

Service terms & payments

Advice is provided in English by email. It is a personal perspective, not therapy, crisis support or regulated professional advice. We may decline requests outside this scope; if you have paid and we decline your request, contact us for a refund.

Quick Advice (€5): one short reply. Personal Advice (€10): a detailed reply plus one additional reply about the same situation within 7 calendar days. Priority Advice (€20): an in-depth reply plus two additional replies about the same situation within 15 calendar days. Follow-up periods begin when we send the first reply. A new, unrelated question requires a new request.

First-reply turnaround targets apply during the service hours shown above, beginning once we receive both your question and confirmed payment. Follow-up replies may take longer. No subscriptions or recurring payments.

We generally do not refund a fully delivered advice service if you agreed to early delivery. Your legal withdrawal and refund rights still apply, including if we cannot deliver the service. If something goes wrong, email contact@anonymadvice.com.
