Common real-life questions

Questions you can ask anonymously

You do not need to phrase your situation perfectly. People often look for honest advice when they are embarrassed, afraid of being judged, or simply want a neutral opinion before acting.

  • “Should I stay in this relationship or am I ignoring signs that it is not working?”
  • “I received a better job offer, but I am scared I will regret leaving. How should I think about it?”
  • “Was I wrong in this argument, or am I being manipulated into feeling guilty?”
  • “I feel behind everyone else in life. What should I focus on first?”
  • “My PhD is making me miserable. How do I know whether I should continue or change direction?”
  • “I want to tell someone how I feel, but I am afraid it will damage the relationship. What should I consider?”
A second perspective

What “honest advice” means here

Honest advice does not mean telling you what you want to hear or pretending there is one perfect answer. The aim is to help you notice trade-offs, assumptions, boundaries, risks and next steps that may be difficult to see when you are emotionally close to the situation.

For the free tool, the response is intentionally simple: it is an automated reflection based on the topic and mood you select. It does not diagnose you and it does not know facts you have not provided. The paid human option is intended for people who want a longer, more contextual response.

Frequently asked questions

Anonymous advice FAQ

Can I ask for advice completely anonymously?

You do not need an account for the free reflection and you can leave out your name and identifying details. If you choose the human email reply, an email address is needed so a response can be sent back to you.

Is the free response really AI?

The current free version is an automated reflection tool built into the page. It selects relevant reflection prompts based on the topic and mood you choose; it is not a conversational AI model reading and analyzing your full story.

What kind of advice can I ask for?

You can ask for general perspective on relationships, work, career, life direction, difficult decisions, motivation, loneliness, grief, academic pressure and many other everyday personal situations.

How does the €10 human advice option work?

Choose the human option, pay through PayPal, then use the email button to send your request. The target response time is 24–72 hours excluding weekends and holidays.

Is this therapy, medical advice or legal advice?

No. AnonymAdvice provides general reflective support and personal perspective only. It does not replace licensed medical, mental-health, legal, financial or emergency services.

What should I do if I am in immediate danger or crisis?

Do not rely on this website for emergency support. Contact the emergency services or an appropriate professional crisis service in your location.
